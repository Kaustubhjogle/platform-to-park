# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js with TypeScript and Tailwind CSS, selected by the user.

## Users

Mumbai Metro and local-rail commuters with a short break who want a simple reason to step outdoors.

## Product Purpose

Platform to Park turns a transit stop into a short, map-informed nature mission built from open map and biodiversity data.

## Positioning

It connects a station, a mapped public green space, and species observed nearby into one concise outdoor prompt.

## Operating Context

Users use the app on a phone before or after a commute. The screen should be the shortest part of the experience.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The MVP uses curated demo data. Later, OpenStreetMap, iNaturalist, and on-device open-weight identification will replace it. It must not make unverified safety, access, or opening-hours claims.

## Evidence on Hand

Official MMRDA station lists, OpenStreetMap, and the iNaturalist public API are intended data sources. No live feeds or user accounts are required for the MVP.

## Product Principles

- Make leaving the screen feel easier than staying on it.
- Attribute open data and state uncertainty plainly.
- Prefer a small, actionable mission over a long itinerary.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use clear language, keyboard-operable controls, visible focus states, and do not imply routes are accessible or safe without verified data.
